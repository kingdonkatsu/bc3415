# Curriculum insights (sample - to be verified by LCentral academic team)

## Primary English composition
Strong compositions usually come from planning before writing: deciding the main event, the problem and how it is resolved. Many students lose marks because they start writing without a plan.

## Primary English comprehension
Comprehension answers are often marked down for lifting whole sentences from the passage instead of answering the exact question asked.

## PSLE English writing paper
The PSLE English writing paper has two parts: Situational Writing and Continuous Writing.

## Upper Primary Maths word problems
From P5, many students meet heavier multi-step word problems involving fractions, ratio and percentage. Drawing models and checking units help them avoid careless errors.

## Primary Science open-ended questions
Open-ended Science questions reward precise use of keywords and a clear cause-and-effect explanation.

## Secondary A Maths
A Maths builds heavily on algebraic manipulation. Gaps in Sec 1 and Sec 2 algebra are a common reason students find A Maths difficult in Sec 3.

## Secondary Chemistry
Students often find mole calculations and writing balanced equations challenging; regular timed practice helps.
