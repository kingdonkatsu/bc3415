# Seasonal programmes (sample - dates and prices to be confirmed by LCentral)

## Year-end holiday programme
During the November and December school holidays the Centre runs short holiday workshops to help students consolidate the year's topics and prepare for the next level. Dates and fees are confirmed closer to the holidays; ask the CST for details.

## Head start for the new school year
The year-end period is a good time to start, so the child settles into the class routine before the new school year begins.
