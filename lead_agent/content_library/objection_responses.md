# Approved responses to common concerns

## Concern: price or budget
Acknowledge the concern. Explain what is included: small groups of at most 8, weekly marked homework, a topical test every 4 weeks with a progress update to parents. Mention the sibling or multi-subject discount only if it applies.

## Concern: needs to discuss with spouse or family
Offer to send a short summary the parent can share at home: the child's needs, the recommended class and timing, and the next step. Ask when would be a good time to reconnect.

## Concern: timing does not suit
Offer the other classes of the same subject and level that still have seats. If none suit, note the preferred timing so the Centre can inform the parent if a suitable slot opens.

## Concern: child already has a tutor or attends another centre
Do not criticise other providers. Offer the diagnostic assessment as a no-pressure way to see whether there are gaps the current arrangement is not covering.

## Concern: unsure whether the child needs tuition
Suggest the 20-minute diagnostic assessment so the decision is based on the child's actual gaps.
