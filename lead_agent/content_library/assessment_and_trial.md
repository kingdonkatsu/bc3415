# Assessment and trial process

## Diagnostic assessment
New students can take a short 20-minute diagnostic assessment so the tutor can understand the child's current level and gaps. There is no entrance test and no student is turned away because of their grades.

## What parents receive after the assessment
After the assessment, the Centre shares what the child did well, the main gaps observed, and a recommended class.

## Trial lesson
A trial lesson costs $30. The $30 is deducted from the first month's fees if the student enrols within 7 days of the trial. Each student can have one trial per subject. Parents may observe the first 15 minutes of the trial lesson.

## How to enrol
After the trial, the parent tells the Centre which class to enrol into. Enrolment needs the one-time $50 registration fee and a refundable deposit of one month's fees per subject.
