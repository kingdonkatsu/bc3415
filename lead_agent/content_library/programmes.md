# Programmes

## Primary programme (P3 to P6)
Subjects: Maths, Science and English. Small groups of at most 8 students, one 2-hour lesson a week, weekly homework and a topical test every 4 weeks. Parents receive a short progress update after every topical test.

## P6 PSLE preparation
From Term 2, P6 classes focus on PSLE preparation, including timed practice papers.

## Lower Secondary programme (Sec 1 to Sec 2)
Subjects: Maths, Science and English, in small groups of at most 8.

## Upper Secondary programme (Sec 3 to Sec 4)
Subjects: E Maths, A Maths, Physics, Chemistry and English. From Term 2, Sec 4 classes focus on O-Level preparation.

## English composition and comprehension focus
Our English classes are taught by a tutor who specialises in composition and comprehension, with 8 years of experience.
